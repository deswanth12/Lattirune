## 1. CHAPTER OBJECTIVES
1. **Primary Narrative Objective:** Return to the Remnant Safehouse, treat Ren's injuries, and investigate the civilian research permit belonging to Dr. Donald Vane.
2. **Primary Gameplay Objective:** Undergo Aura Calibration with Walter, restore power to the safehouse drainage sump, defeat tracking units, and decrypt the sealed *VANE_HOME_2014* archive.
