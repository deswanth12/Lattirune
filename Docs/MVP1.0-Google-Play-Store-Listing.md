## 3. Full Description

**Align the Lattice. Awaken the Runes.**

Lattirune is a portrait-mode 2D spatial inventory auto-battler roguelite where your tactical puzzle layout directly dictates your combat might. Position directional elemental runes across a 5×5 lattice grid to project continuous energy conduits through your weapons, shields, and relics—triggering devastating, compounding chain reactions against sinister dungeon horrors!

### ⚔️ KEY FEATURES

* **TACTICAL 5×5 LATTICE COMBAT**  
  Every tile matters. Arrange weapons, shields, and directional runes across the discrete grid to optimize conduit paths, power adjacent armaments, and maximize synergies.

* **DIRECTIONAL RUNE CONDUITS & ELEMENTAL REACTIONS**  
  Direct beams of Fire, Ice, Lightning, Poison, and Light across your equipment. Intersect distinct elemental conduits to ignite Steam blinders, Plasma rays, Toxic Flame detonations, Superconductor resistance shredding, and Frostbite damage amplification!

* **OPTICAL PRISMS & MULTI-DIRECTIONAL EMITTERS**  
  Bend and multiply your power with optical Prism splitters and 4-way Crossfire emitters, crafting intricate web networks of compounding damage.

* **10-FLOOR CURSED SEWERS ROGUELITE RUNS**  
  Venture through Biome 1 across 10 deterministic challenge floors. Face goblin ambushes, acidic slimes, damage-reflecting skeletons, minion-summoning necromancers, and the formidable 3-phase Lich Lord boss!

* **SPATIAL BACKPACK INVENTORY**  
  Manage your limited inventory grid, rotate multi-tile equipment, and purchase tactical backpack expansions at floor merchant stalls.

* **CAMPFIRE SITES & IN-RUN ECONOMY**  
  Earn in-run Gold from defeated monsters to buy rare weapons and runes at merchant stalls. Rest at Floor 8 campfires to choose between restoring health or reforging an active rune.

* **PERSISTENT META-PROGRESSION & BLUEPRINT FORGE**  
  Defeat bosses and earn persistent Dungeon Embers. Unlock 12 permanent game-changing blueprints in the Blueprint Forge to empower your hero for future dungeon expeditions.

* **DESIGNED FOR MOBILE & 100% OFFLINE**  
  Enjoy seamless portrait one-handed play with responsive touch controls, instant speed multipliers (1x, 2x, 3x), tactile haptic feedback, and complete offline playability—zero ads, zero microtransactions, and zero network required!
