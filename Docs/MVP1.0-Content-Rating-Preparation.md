# Lattirune MVP 1.0 Content Rating Preparation Document

**Date:** August 19, 2026  
**Application:** Lattirune  
**Target Package:** `com.developer.lattirune`  
**Evaluation Standard:** IARC (International Age Rating Coalition) via Google Play Console  
**Disclaimer:** *Final rating must be completed through the official Google Play questionnaire.*

---

## 1. Violence & Combat Characteristics

* **Combat Context:** Stylized fantasy tactical auto-battler combat against mythical monsters (rats, goblins, skeletons, spiders, slimes, necromancers, and an undead Lich Lord).
* **Visual Depiction:** 2D pixel/vector stylized sprite animations. Defeated combatants fade out or play simple disintegration animations.
* **Blood & Gore:** **Zero.** No graphic depictions of decapitation, dismemberment, realistic blood splatters, or human suffering.
* **Weaponry:** Stylized fantasy swords, daggers, shortbows, shields, and magic wands.
* **Elemental Effects:** Stylized particle bursts representing fire burn, ice chill, shock spark, and poison status effects.

---

## 2. Fear, Horror & Tone

* **Themes:** Fantasy dungeon crawler atmosphere ("The Cursed Sewers").
* **Sensory Intensity:** Mild suspense during boss battle encounters with atmospheric dark fantasy audio cues. Zero jump scares or disturbing psychological horror elements.

---

## 3. Controlled Substances, Language & Nudity

* **Profanity & Crudity:** **Zero.** No coarse language, profanity, or sexually suggestive dialogue.
* **Substances:** Fantasy consumable potions (Health Potion, Stamina Flask, Poison Vial). Zero depictions of real-world drugs, alcohol, or tobacco products.
* **Nudity & Sexual Content:** **Zero.** All characters and monsters wear stylized fantasy armor or robes.

---

## 4. Monetization & Gambling Invariants

* **Real Money Gambling:** **None.**
* **Paid Loot Boxes / Gacha:** **None.** All 3-card reward drafts are earned purely through in-game gameplay progression using non-purchasable in-run currencies.
* **In-App Purchases:** **None.**

---

## 5. User Interaction & Online Features

* **User Location Sharing:** **None.**
* **User-Generated Content:** **None.**
* **Unfiltered Online Chat / Voice:** **None.** Single-player experience only.
* **Personal Data Collection:** **None.**

---

## 6. Expected Target Age Categories (Informational Guideline)

* **ESRB:** Everyone 10+ (Fantasy Violence)
* **PEGI:** PEGI 7 (Mild Fantasy Violence)
* **USK:** USK 6 (Fantasy Combat)
* **ClassInd:** 10 (Mild Violence)
